TXT's Yeonjun Makes PFW Runway Debut at Miu Miu With a Statement Piece

By TeenVogue

TXT member Yeonjun has made his long-awaited return to the modeling world, walking in Miu Miu's fall-winter 2026 show during Paris Fashion Week.

On March 9, one day before the show, Yeonjun had posted a tease for fans on his Instagram account. "See you soon," the star wrote in a post tagging the brand, posing in his Parisian hotel room donning a full look from the brand.

The surprise came on the day of the actual show, when Yeonjun was nowhere to be seen on the VIP arrivals, but rather strutting the pasture catwalk as a model, wearing a look comprised of brown leather shoes, electric blue pants, a pullover shirt with the Miu Miu logo on the chest, and a distinctive winter-style hat with large, fluffy brown fur ear flaps, which is already poised to become a go-to next season.

Yeonjun made his fashion week debut back in 2021 as part of the virtual presentation for Concept Korea. This Paris Fashion Week appearance marks his first time walking a show, and to do so for a major brand like Miu Miu is no small feat.
